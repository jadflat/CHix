# Changelog

## 1.1

The music now plays on a small software FM synth: bell, pluck and arpeggio voices with drums, mixed in an interrupt, with the sound effects over the top. Five new psytrance songs (psychill, goa, full-on, dark psy and forest, from calmest to most frantic), each with its own sound and a sweeping FM index. Still about 16 ms a frame.

## 1.0

First release: 25 stages with ten detailed cityscapes drawn per pixel, eight main enemies, ten power-ups, an acid-techno soundtrack, a pause menu and a saved game. About 16 ms a frame on the board.
